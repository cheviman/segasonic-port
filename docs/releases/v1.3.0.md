The native port of Sega's 1993 arcade game *SegaSonic The Hedgehog*: not an emulator, but the game's own
program decompiled into C ([segasonic-decomp](https://github.com/cheviman/segasonic-decomp)) and built for
Windows, Linux and macOS, and, with its assets made on your computer, for the Nintendo Switch, Android and
Anbernic's handhelds.

**No game data is included.** You need your own MAME ROM set: `sonic.zip`, *SegaSonic The Hedgehog*
(Japan, rev. C). Put it beside the game, or drop it on the window the game opens the first time: the game
checks it and takes out what it uses, once.

| System | Package |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11, x64 | `segasonic-windows-x64.zip` |
| Linux, x64 | `segasonic-linux-x64.tar.gz` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon and Intel | `segasonic-macos-universal.zip` |
| Nintendo Switch | made by `SegaSonic NSP`, in each package above |
| Android 7 or later | made by `SegaSonic APK`, in each package above |
| Anbernic's H700 handhelds, stock firmware | `segasonic-anbernic.zip` |

What is new since v1.2.2, on the computers (asked for and reported in
[#2](https://github.com/cheviman/segasonic-port/issues/2)):

- **OPTIONS → TRACKBALL** (SegaSonic): how far the controller's stick, the keys and the pad's cross and the
  mouse roll Sonic, set while you move him around the how to play's floor. Up and down choose a setting,
  left and right change it, accept tries it (Sonic moves and jumps until back): STICK and KEYS, MOUSE (as F5
  and F6), ROLLS ON (as F4), and DRAG and FRICTION for the mouse's ball. They are kept in `segasonic.ini`.
- **The test mode** (F2) in SegaSonic: up and down move its cursor and Enter picks, as 9 and F2 do. Back no
  longer leaves EXIT and the lines under it blank.
- **Game controllers SDL does not know**, such as a generic USB pad, in both games: put their mappings in
  `gamecontrollerdb.txt` where the game keeps its files (the lines of
  [SDL_GameControllerDB](https://github.com/mdqinc/SDL_GameControllerDB), or the one a gamepad tool makes),
  and the game takes them as it starts, without Steam.

The Switch, Android and Anbernic's handhelds play as in v1.2.2: an `.nsp` or an APK made with v1.2.2's
`SegaSonic NSP` and `SegaSonic APK` needs no making again. What they make, and the Anbernic folder with your
pack in it, carries your ROM's assets: it is yours alone, never share it.

SDL2 and the C runtime are built into the computers' executables: nothing else to install. The macOS apps
are not signed by a developer: the first time, open one with right click → Open (on macOS 15 and later, try
to open it, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway).

The game was checked frame by frame against MAME, picture and sound, on Windows and Linux, and played on a
Mac too.
