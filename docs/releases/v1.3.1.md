The native port of Sega's 1993 arcade game *SegaSonic The Hedgehog*: not an emulator, but the game's own
program decompiled into C ([segasonic-decomp](https://github.com/cheviman/segasonic-decomp)) and built for
Windows, Linux and macOS, and, with its assets made on your computer, for the Nintendo Switch, Android and
Anbernic's handhelds.

**No game data is included.** You need your own MAME ROM set: `sonic.zip`, *SegaSonic The Hedgehog*
(Japan, rev. C). Put it beside the game, or drop it on the window the game opens the first time: the game
checks it and takes out what it uses, once.

| System | Package |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11, x64 | `segasonic-windows-x64.zip` |
| Linux, x64 | `segasonic-linux-x64.tar.gz` |
| macOS 11 or later, Apple silicon and Intel | `segasonic-macos-universal.zip` |
| Nintendo Switch | made by `SegaSonic NSP`, in each package above |
| Android 7 or later | made by `SegaSonic APK`, in each package above |
| Anbernic's H700 handhelds, stock firmware | `segasonic-anbernic.zip` |

What is new since v1.3.0:

- **Android on a foldable**: on a screen tall enough for the game's own 4:3 picture with room left under it,
  as an open Galaxy Z Fold's, that picture goes at the top, larger than the 16:9 one, and the touch controls
  under it; closed, the 16:9 picture as before. Folding or unfolding mid-game changes it at once. The touch
  controls are the same size on every phone now (A and START were enormous on the open Fold), and the stick
  is a fifth larger.
- **Around the picture**, in the console version on every system: where the picture leaves the screen (full
  screen on a computer, the scenes between bars in WIDE, the room under the picture on an open foldable, a
  wide phone's sides), OPTIONS' Sonic heads stand still instead of black.
- **One trackball a mouse on Windows** ([#3](https://github.com/cheviman/segasonic-port/pull/3), by
  knuckleslee): each mouse, or trackball, is a player's trackball. The first one to move is player 1's, and
  the next ones to press a button are players 2 and 3, each with its own left button (the button) and right
  button (which holds the ball). `separate = no` under `[mouse]` in the game's ini file makes the mouse
  player 1's alone, as on the other systems.

The Switch's `.nsp` and the Android APK are made again with this release's `SegaSonic NSP` and
`SegaSonic APK` (their `switch` and `android` folders hold the new game); an APK made with the same key
installs over the last one and keeps what the game wrote. What they make, and the Anbernic folder with your
pack in it, carries your ROM's assets: it is yours alone, never share it.

SDL2 and the C runtime are built into the computers' executables: nothing else to install. The macOS apps
are not signed by a developer: the first time, open one with right click → Open (on macOS 15 and later, try
to open it, then System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway).

The game was checked frame by frame against MAME, picture and sound, on Windows and Linux, and played on a
Mac too.
